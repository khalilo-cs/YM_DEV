# Project Overview

**YEMENDEV AI** - Global Autonomous Digital Resilience & Evolution Platform.
*Understand. Predict. Protect. Recover. Evolve.*

## In one sentence

A platform that repairs software with AI assistance **safely**: it understands a project, reproduces
its failures in isolation, proposes and simulates repairs, applies them only with a *different*
human's approval, verifies them independently, rolls back byte-exactly on failure, keeps tamper-evident
evidence of every step, learns reusable skills, and keeps working without internet or through power loss.

## Core idea

The system is designed on the assumption that **models, tools and people make mistakes**. Model
output is untrusted input; nothing counts as fixed until an independent check says so.

## Who it is for

Software companies maintaining legacy systems; organisations with critical systems and unreliable
connectivity; universities (teaching and reproducible research); individual developers.

## What exists today (0.1.0)

The verified core: scanning, sandboxed test execution (process or Docker), diagnostics, repair with
simulation / approval / verification / rollback, skills and learning, persistent resumable tasks,
governance (roles, project memberships, bound approvals), tamper-evident audit and evidence, agents
with fixed permissions, a background task queue with scheduling, retries and timeouts, a predictive risk engine with causal analysis, a digital immune system, a
counterfactual (what-if) engine, signed offline synchronization and continuity passports, HTTP API, CLI,
a web UI in Arabic and English, and a resilience benchmark. All 18 MVP criteria are covered by
automated tests. Exact status per feature: [FEATURE_STATUS.md](FEATURE_STATUS.md).

## What does not exist yet

Desktop and mobile apps, JavaScript/TypeScript analysis, PostgreSQL,
network synchronization transport, comparative research baselines, and evaluation with a real model.
